01 - Introduction

About this policy.

This Privacy Policy applies to Tower Balance 3D (the "App"), a free mobile game developed and published by Funzar Labs ("we," "us," "our") on Google Play. It explains what information is collected when you use the App, what it is used for, and the choices you have.

Tower Balance 3D is a casual stacking game rated for 3+ on Google Play. The App is free to download and play, supported by advertising. There are no in-app purchases.

By downloading, installing, or using the App, you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

Quick summary

Tower Balance 3D collects only what is needed to run the game, show ads, and send optional notifications. Your game progress is stored on your device. We don't sell your personal data, and we never ask for your name, email, or location. You can turn off personalized ads and notifications any time in your device settings.

02 - Data Safety

Google Play Data Safety summary.

The following reflects the Data Safety section shown on our Google Play listing - what the App collects, why, and whether it is shared.

Device or other IDs
Advertising ID (GAID) and similar identifiers - used for showing ads and ad measurement. Shared with our ad partners.
Ad interactions
Which ads were shown, viewed, or clicked - used by our ad partners for ad delivery, frequency capping, and measurement.
Push token
If you allow notifications, a push token is created so we can send occasional game updates. It does not identify you personally.

Data is encrypted in transit. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, physical address, photos, contacts, health data, financial information, or precise location. Your gameplay progress and scores are stored locally on your device. We do not sell your personal information, and the App does not offer in-app purchases.

03 - Information We Collect

What we collect.

Automatically collected

  • Device identifiers - your Advertising ID (GAID), a resettable identifier provided by Google Play services, and similar device identifiers.
  • Device & system information - device model, Android version, language, region, and screen size, used by ad partners to serve compatible ads.
  • Ad interaction data - which ads were shown, viewed, or clicked, used for ad measurement and to avoid repeating the same ad.
  • Push notification token - created only if you grant notification permission.

Stored on your device only

  • Gameplay data - your scores, high score, coins, unlocked skins, and settings are saved locally on your device (and in the device's app storage backup). They are not uploaded to our servers.

We do not collect

  • Your name, email address, or phone number.
  • Your precise GPS location.
  • Your contacts, photos, calendar, or microphone data.
  • Health, fitness, biometric, or sensitive personal information.
  • Payment information - the App has no in-app purchases.
04 - How We Use It

How we use your information.

  • Operate the App and save your game progress on your device.
  • Show ads inside the App and measure their effectiveness.
  • Send optional push notifications about game updates (only with your permission).
  • Detect, prevent, and address fraud, abuse, or technical issues.
  • Comply with legal obligations.
05 - Third Parties

Third-party services we use.

Tower Balance 3D uses the following third-party services. Each handles your data under its own privacy policy:

Google Play

Google distributes the App and processes downloads and updates. See the Google Privacy Policy.

CleverAdsSolutions (CAS.AI)

We use CAS as our ad mediation platform. CAS shows ads from multiple ad networks (such as Google AdMob, Meta Audience Network, Unity Ads, AppLovin, ironSource, and others) and may collect device identifiers and ad interaction data. See the CAS Privacy Policy and the policies of the underlying ad networks.

Google Firebase (Cloud Messaging)

We use Firebase Cloud Messaging only to deliver push notifications you have opted into. Firebase processes a push token and basic device information for delivery. See Firebase Privacy & Security.

Google Play services

Provides the Advertising ID and ad-measurement APIs used to attribute ad campaigns without identifying you personally. See the Google Privacy Policy.

These providers may use SDKs and device identifiers solely to perform the services we engage them for. We do not sell your personal data to any of them.

06 - Advertising ID

Advertising ID and your choices.

On Android, advertising is based on your device's Advertising ID (GAID) - a resettable identifier provided by Google Play services. The App and its ad partners may use it to show ads and measure their performance.

  • Reset your Advertising ID: open Settings → Privacy → Ads (on some devices Settings → Google → Ads) and tap "Reset advertising ID".
  • Opt out of personalized ads: in the same screen, choose "Delete advertising ID" or "Opt out of Ads Personalization." You will still see ads, but they will not be personalized. The game works exactly the same.

These controls are provided by Android and Google. Menu names vary slightly by device and Android version.

07 - Advertising

How advertising works.

Tower Balance 3D is free to play. To support development, we show ads inside the App:

  • Banner ads - a small ad shown at the bottom of the screen.
  • Interstitial ads - a full-screen ad shown at natural break points, such as between rounds.
  • Rewarded ads - optional ads you can choose to watch in exchange for in-game benefits (such as continuing your tower after a miss).

Ads are served through CleverAdsSolutions (CAS.AI), which mediates between multiple ad networks. Ad content is controlled by these networks, not by Funzar Labs. We work to keep ads age-appropriate, but cannot guarantee every ad will match every player's preferences.

If you see an inappropriate ad, please report it to hello@funzarlabs.com with a screenshot if possible - we'll investigate with our ad partners.

08 - Notifications

Push notifications.

The App may ask permission to send push notifications - for example, occasional reminders or news about updates. Notifications are entirely optional:

  • Decline the permission when first asked, and no notifications will ever be sent.
  • Turn them off later at any time in Settings → Notifications → Tower Balance 3D.

Notification delivery uses Firebase Cloud Messaging. The push token created for delivery is not used for advertising.

09 - Children

Children's privacy.

Tower Balance 3D is rated for 3+ on Google Play and may be enjoyed by players of all ages. We design the App to be family-friendly:

  • No in-app purchases - children cannot accidentally spend money.
  • No social features - no chat, messaging, or user-generated content.
  • No personal data collection - we never ask for names, emails, or phone numbers.

We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country) without verifiable parental consent, as required by COPPA and similar laws. If you believe your child has provided personal information, contact hello@funzarlabs.com and we will delete it promptly.

Parental controls

Android and Google Play offer parental controls through Google Family Link and Google Play's content settings. We recommend using these to manage your child's app usage.

10 - Your Rights

Your rights & choices.

  • Access / Delete - request information about, or deletion of, data linked to your device by emailing us.
  • Limit ad tracking - reset your Advertising ID or opt out of Ads Personalization in device settings (see Section 06).
  • Notifications - disable any time in device settings.
  • Delete the App - uninstalling stops all further data collection from your device.

To exercise these rights, email hello@funzarlabs.com. We respond within 30 days.

For users in the EU / UK (GDPR)

You additionally have rights to data portability, restriction of processing, objection, and to lodge a complaint with your local authority. Our legal bases are consent (advertising, notifications) and legitimate interest (fraud prevention, service operation). A consent dialog is shown where required by law.

For users in California (CCPA / CPRA)

California residents may know, delete, and opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. We do not sell personal information. Sharing of device identifiers for advertising may be considered "sharing" under CPRA - you can opt out using the controls in Section 06.

11 - Security

How we protect your data.

All network requests use encrypted connections (HTTPS / TLS). We keep our SDKs and dependencies up to date and review them regularly. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure; if a security incident affects your data, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required by law.

12 - Retention

How long data is kept.

  • Gameplay data - stays on your device; removed when you uninstall the App or clear its data.
  • Ad interaction data - retained by CAS and the underlying ad networks per their policies (typically 90-180 days).
  • Push tokens - invalidated when you uninstall the App or disable notifications.
  • Device identifiers - deleted from ad systems when you reset your Advertising ID or uninstall the App.
